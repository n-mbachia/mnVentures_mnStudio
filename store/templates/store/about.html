About MN Studio

MN Studio specializes in crafting custom and bespoke furniture using African hardwoods, softwoods, salvaged timber, and upcycled materials. We blend natural elements with innovative design to create functional, timeless pieces—ranging from live-edge furniture to integrated material decor.
Our work serves a diverse clientele, from first-time homeowners to large commercial spaces, with over 65% of our projects tailored to specific client needs. Every piece is made to order, ensuring you get exactly what you envision, finished to the highest standard.
